🌱 About My Decision Tree

"My Decision Tree" is an interactive website that shows how different financial situations can lead to different decisions that may be good or bad and develop moral and spiritual values in life.

Each branch represents a life situations that happen in reality, while the financial decisions represent possible actions we are going to make every life situations. The fruits represent the values that can guide us in making responsible and meaningful choices.

🌸 Values / Spiritual Values

🙏 Gratitude. It is showing appreciation for the kindness, help, or good things that happen in your life.
❤️ Generosity.The quality of being kind and generous.
🌿 Prudence. A skill and good judgment in the management of affairs and the use of resources.
🍃 Temperance. The quality of moderation or self-restraint.
🌳 Responsibility. State or fact of having a duty to deal with something or of having control over someone.
🕊️ Detachment. The state of being objective or aloof.
✝️ Trust in God. Trusting God for His plans.
🤝 Service to Others. It involves putting the needs of others before your own and acting with genuine care and empathy.
💛 Faith. Complete trust or confidence in someone or something.

📖 What We Have Learned

Making decisions involving money is not easy. In buying things, we need to think about our priorities, the events, the struggles, and we need to think for the common good. We need to budget our money, because every cent are hard to get and find. And we also realized that as we get older, money isn't just a paper or a metal that we need, to buy what we want. It is the blood, sweat, and tears of our parents who worked hard to be able to provide for us. And as we also get older, we are becoming more aware that budgeting and prioritizing our needs over our wants are important.
